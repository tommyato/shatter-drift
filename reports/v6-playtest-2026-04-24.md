## Qualitative Observations

### Shatter Mechanic
- Agent dies in SHATTER mode on all 3 runs — it actively holds shatter into late-game obstacles.
- Mid-run challenge screen observed: "Phase through 15 walls: 8/10" — confirming wall-phasing is happening, not just being avoided.
- Shatter ring visually confirmed in screenshots at 43m, 256m, 307m, 340m.
- Agent does NOT spam shatter (high combos prove it releases/recharges correctly).

### Centerline Behavior
- `run1-mid.png` (307m): Agent threads exactly through center of a wide wall gap, x8 combo active.
- `run1-gameplay.png` (256m): Agent splits between two purple pillars via center lane.
- Both are consistent with the v6 reward shape `is_shattered * dist_to_center` — agent learned to center when shattered.
- In solid mode, agent makes lateral dodges to avoid obstacles then returns toward center.

### Smoothness
- Combos of x13, x14, x16 are impossible to build with jitter or freeze.
- NEAR MISS bonuses logged across multiple screenshots — agent grazes obstacles at high speed without collision.
- No observed rapid-toggle, wall-hug, or stuck-in-one-lane behavior.

### Death Pattern
- Runs 2 and 3 both died when SPEEDBOOST powerup was active — the agent collected speed boost, game speed spiked, and it couldn't adjust quickly enough.
- Run 1 died at 492m during a Boss Wave (boss parts added dynamically outside normal obstacle list; agent may not see boss parts correctly via the bossObstacles merge code).
- All 3 deaths at 27–36 m/s: high-speed zone where reaction time window is tiny.

### Zone Consistency
- 3/3 runs reached Crystal Caves — a consistent late-game biome. The agent is not dying in early zones.

## Root Cause of Borderline Performance

1. **Training cut at 89.7%** — OOM at 4.49M/5M steps means the reward signal (positional phase) had not fully converged. The 52–55 mean_ret plateau is consistent with near-but-not-optimal policy.
2. **Boss Wave blind spot** — The run1 death at 492m in a Boss Wave is notable. Boss parts are merged dynamically in `game.ts:1237–1251` but may not be visible to the ONNX agent with sufficient lead time (boss parts are animated THREE.js objects at close range).
3. **Speed boost trap** — Runs 2 and 3 died immediately after a SPEEDBOOST powerup. The agent doesn't appear to account for the speed increase; a v7 training curriculum with longer high-speed episodes might fix this.
